Complete list of all key equations  
(from the original handwritten notes)

1. Shannon Entropy
\[
H\bigl(P(\lambda)\bigr)=-\sum_i P(x_i\mid r(\lambda))\log P(x_i\mid r(\lambda))
\]

\[
H\bigl(P(\lambda)\bigr)=-\sum_i P_i\log_2 P_i
\]

2. Infinite sums and integral
\[
\sum_{i=1}^{n}a_i=\sum_{i=1}^{n}\arg\left(\frac{z_{n+1}}{z_n}\right)
\]

\[
\sum_{i=0}^{\infty}a_i=\int_0^1|\sin(2\pi x)|\,dx=\frac{2}{\pi}
\]

\[
\bigcup_{i\in I}A_i=\bigoplus_{i\in I}V_i
\]

3. Limiting argument and phase
\[
\lim_{N\to\infty}\Phi_N=-\arctan(2\pi)\approx-1.4129651365\,\text{rad}\approx-80.9569^\circ
\]

\[
\lim_{t\to 0^+}\arg\bigl(z(t)\bigr)=\arctan(2\pi)\approx1.4129651365\,\text{rad}\approx80.9569^\circ
\]

\[
e^{i\phi_N}=e^{-i\pi}=-1
\]

4. Complex path and half-winding contour
\[
z(t)=t\,(1+2\pi i),\qquad t>0
\]

\[
\frac{1}{2\pi i}\int_{\Gamma_R}\frac{dz}{z}=\frac12
\]

5. Radial temperature schedule
\[
\tau(\lambda)=2\pi(1-\lambda),\qquad\lambda\in[0,1]
\]

6. Polar identification (canonical)
\[
x(\lambda)=\tau(\lambda)\cos t(\lambda)
\]

\[
y(\lambda)=\tau(\lambda)\sin t(\lambda)
\]

\[
z_i=\operatorname{Re}(p_i)=\tau\cos t_i
\]

7. Temperature-scaled Softmax
\[
P_i=\frac{\exp(z_i/\tau)}{\sum_j\exp(z_j/\tau)}
\]

\[
P_i=\frac{\exp(\cos t_i)}{\sum_j\exp(\cos t_j)}\quad\text{(after cancellation)}
\]

8. Attention
\[
A_t=\operatorname{softmax}\left(\frac{Q_tK_t^\top}{\sqrt{d_k}\cdot\tau(\lambda)}\right)
\]

\[
\phi_{\rm wind}(\lambda,n)=\tau(\lambda)+n\delta
\]

9. Angular invariants (gates)
\[
\theta=\arctan(2\pi)\approx1.41296514\,\text{rad}\approx80.9569^\circ
\]

\[
\phi=\arctan(\pi)\approx1.26262726\,\text{rad}\approx72.3432^\circ
\]

\[
\Psi=\theta-\phi\approx0.15033788\,\text{rad}\approx8.6137^\circ
\]

\[
\alpha=\frac12\theta\approx0.70648257\,\text{rad}\approx40.4785^\circ
\]

\[
K_{\rm norm}\approx0.70699
\]

10. Residual and effective angle
\[
\delta_{\rm residual}\approx0.00517\,\text{rad}
\]

\[
\theta_{\rm effective}=\theta_{\rm base}+n\cdot\delta
\]

\[
1-P_t(v^*)\le|V|\,e^{-A_{\min}/\tau}
\]

11. Trigonometric identities of the separation
\[
\sin\Psi\approx0.149772,\qquad\cos\Psi\approx0.998872,\qquad\tan\Psi\approx0.151481
\]
